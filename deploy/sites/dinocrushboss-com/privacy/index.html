Privacy

Dino Crush is a free game. Guest play stores stars only in this browser. If you create an email save we store that email, a hashed password, game progress, scores, and anonymous play telemetry (session length, errors, version) so the board stays fair.

We do not sell a contact list. We do not run a card shop or cash checkout on dinocrushboss.com. There is no guest play on the public game host — an email save is the account.

To request deletion, email from the address on the save, or use login.dinocrushboss.com and write us from that inbox.
