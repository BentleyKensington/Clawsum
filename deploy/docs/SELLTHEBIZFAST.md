## How we present as a qualified buyer

Brokers filter. Lead with a **buyer packet** (one page), not a novel:

1. **Entity:** Hennessey Holdings LLC (holding). Ops brand Clawsum is not the buyer of record unless Gerald says otherwise.
2. **Thesis:** 2–4 sentences matching *this* listing (industry, geo, why we can operate/add).
3. **Proof of funds / finance path:** SBA prequal letter, liquidity range **bands** (not account numbers in chat), or “seller-finance comfortable at X%.” Exact dollars only in the packet Gerald approved.
4. **Team:** operator (who runs it day 1), CPA, attorney — names when real.
5. **Timeline:** “CIM this week, IOI in 10 days, exclusivity if numbers hold.”
6. **NDA:** sign immediately; ask for CIM / CIM-lite / P&L + tax returns (3 years) / customer concentration / owner hours.

Phrases that work (plain, not hype):

- “We’re a searcher with a defined buy box and financing path — not browsing.”
- “Happy to NDA today. Could you send the CIM or a one-pager plus TTM SDE?”
- “If it’s outside [revenue band / geo / owner-hours], we’ll pass quickly so you can move on.”

Avoid: “no money down” as an opener (Carl Allen teaches structure *after* rapport; leading with it reads as unserious). Avoid: pretending we already own ten companies.

## Question ladder

### A. Overview (first call / first email)

1. Why is the owner selling, and what does success look like for them after close?
2. Owner hours now vs. what a replacement GM would do.
3. Recurring vs. project mix; top 10 customers as % of revenue (bands OK pre-CIM).
4. Last 3 years revenue and SDE/EBITDA **direction** (up / flat / down).
5. Staff count, key-person risk, whether the owner is the rainmaker.
6. Real estate: owned or leased; remaining term.
7. What would you fix if you kept it 24 months?

### B. Trench (after CIM / NDA)

**Earnings:** monthly P&L 24–36 months; add-backs listed and *evidenced*; owner perks vs. true SDE; seasonality; pipeline vs. booked.

**Expenses:** COGS drivers; labor %; insurance; software stack; any related-party rent.

**Staff:** org chart; tenure; who stays; non-competes; what the owner still touches daily.

**Customers:** concentration; contract vs. handshake; churn; NPS/complaints.

**Legal/ops:** licenses, vehicles, equipment condition, environmental, pending claims.

**Working capital:** AR quality, AP, inventory if any, seasonality peg.

Never request raw bank logins. Never store CIMs in Discord. MinIO + Obsidian `SellTheBizFast/deals/<slug>/` only.
